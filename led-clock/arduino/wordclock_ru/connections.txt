Power (S-25-5, 5V 5A — feeds BOTH the strip and the ESP32):
  PSU V+ (5V)  → strip 5V  AND  ESP32 "5V" pin
  PSU V- (GND) → strip GND AND  ESP32 "GND" pin   (single common ground)
  ESP32 GPIO48 → [330 ohm] → strip DIN (data)

Critical notes:
- Ground MUST be a solid short direct wire (NOT a breadboard jumper). A floating
  ground reference caused "green snow"/brightness jumps — fixed by a real GND wire.
- Do NOT power via USB and external 5V at the same time: the DevKitC-1 5V pin and
  USB 5V are not diode-isolated -> they short together. Flash via USB only (external
  5V off); run on external 5V only (USB out).
- Never feed 5V into the "3V3" pin — it destroys the board. 5V goes to the "5V" pin.
- If powering the ESP32 from a separate USB charger instead, its GND must still tie
  to the strip/PSU GND, or the green-snow corruption returns.
